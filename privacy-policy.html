Flipadoodle Privacy Policy

Sachin Mukherjee · Effective 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Flipadoodle app for iPhone, iPad and Android uses, and why. Flipadoodle is made by Sachin Mukherjee, an independent developer ("we").

What the game stores on your device

Your progress, ink, upgrades, weapons and settings are saved only on your device. We don't run servers for the game, we don't ask you to create an account, and we don't receive this information. Your device's own backups (iCloud or Google) may include it, but we never see it. Deleting the app deletes it from your device.

Advertising

The free version of Flipadoodle shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • How you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs, app launch time and performance
  • Device identifiers: on iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, plus identifiers limited to this app; on Android, the advertising ID

We limit ads to family-friendly content. This data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad. The app asks whether it may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier isn't shared and you still see ads, just less personalised ones. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent. If you're in a region where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the UK or some US states, the app asks for your choices before ads are personalised. You can change them at any time with Privacy choices on the title screen.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Privacy > Ads.

Your rights

Because we don't collect or store your personal information, we have nothing about you to access, correct or delete. To control the information Google uses for ads, use Privacy choices in the game, your phone's tracking and ad settings, or the tools described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can also contact us with any privacy question, and you have the right to complain to your local privacy regulator.

Children

Flipadoodle is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
