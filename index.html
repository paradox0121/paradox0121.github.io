Support

Found a bug, lost your progress, or have an idea? Email sachinmukherjee05@gmail.com. Please include your device model (for example, iPhone 13) and what happened.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress, unless your device's own backup restores it.
  • How do I change my ad privacy choices? Tap Privacy choices on the title screen. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Why did my weapon disappear? Weapons wear out after a few hits or seconds, and ammo runs out. Grab the next one that drops.
